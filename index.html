Thresholds explained
Touch
A stock is checked when its price comes within 0.2% of its lower-band level p* (BB121, 3σ, hourly; p* uses the last 120 completed hourly closes). Each setup gets one chance per anchor, in its first touch hour.
C
Enter if the dip score is at least 0.526 (the frozen 2023–24 median). The score blends the market-driven part of the drop, NIFTY's place in its band and sector strength; breadth too, when known.
Challenger
C, but skip when index insurance is cheap (VIX / NIFTY 20-day volatility at or below the frozen cut, 1.29 for 2026) and NIFTY's 50-day average is falling.
VIX opening guard
VIX is ignored before 09:16 because its first print repeats yesterday's level. Until then the challenger waits.
Hour-close safety net
